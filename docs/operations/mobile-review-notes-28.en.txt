PickChick is the official ordering app of one PickChick fried-chicken restaurant in Almaty, Kazakhstan. Customers browse the menu, customise combos, build a basket, pay with Kaspi.kz and collect the food at the restaurant counter (dine in or take away; no delivery). The app is available in Russian, Kazakh and English (Profile > Language).

SIGN-IN FOR APP REVIEW
1. Tap the profile icon (top right), then "With your phone number".
2. Enter the 10 digits from the Username field (+7 is already shown) and tap Continue. Tapping Continue accepts the terms.
3. On the code screen enter the 4-digit code from the Password field.
4. On "Let's get to know you" tap Done. The name and the other fields are optional.
No SMS, Telegram or WhatsApp message is needed for this review number. Regular customers receive their code in Telegram or WhatsApp. Sign-in is by phone number only; there is no third-party or social login. The menu, the basket and the mini-games (Events tab) also work without signing in.

WHAT THE REVIEW ACCOUNT CAN DO
Browse the full menu, open dishes, customise combos, add and change items in the basket, see the total, open the payment step, view Orders, play the mini-games, edit the profile and delete the account.

WHY REAL ORDERS ARE TURNED OFF FOR THE REVIEW ACCOUNT
Every real order goes to the kitchen of a working restaurant and creates a real Kaspi.kz bank invoice, and Kaspi.kz payment needs a Kaspi.kz account in Kazakhstan. So ordering is turned off for the review account: on the payment step the app says so and offers "How ordering works". Nothing is sent to the kitchen or the bank.
To see the whole order journey without payment, tap "How ordering works" there, or add any dish to the basket and open Profile > Settings (gear icon) > "How ordering works". It uses the items in your basket and shows every stage: payment, preparation, ready, collection.

PAYMENT
Payment is by Kaspi.kz only, the main payment app in Kazakhstan. The customer swipes to pay, receives an invoice in the Kaspi.kz app and confirms it there; the app then shows the order status. The app sells food (physical goods consumed outside the app), so In-App Purchase does not apply (Guideline 3.1.3(e)). There are no digital goods or subscriptions. Prices are the restaurant's current menu prices.

OPENING HOURS
The restaurant takes orders from 10:00 to 00:00 Almaty time (UTC+5), which is 05:00-19:00 UTC / 22:00 on the previous day to 12:00 (PDT, UTC-7, for 29 October 2026) Pacific time. Outside these hours the app shows "Opening at 10:00" and the pay button reads "Closed for now"; the menu, basket, demonstration, games and profile keep working.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile > Manage data > Delete account. The app confirms "Account deleted". The review number can sign in again afterwards and gets a new, empty profile.
