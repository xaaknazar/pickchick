# Pick Box - original local sound effects

Six deterministic mono PCM WAV effects, 22,050 Hz, 16 bit, generated by
`scripts/mobile/pick-box-sounds.py` on 13 November 2026. No recorded or
third-party samples. Seed: `pick-box-1113-<name>`.

- drop: 0.13 s, short rope/cardboard release
- impact: 0.18 s, soft cardboard contact
- perfect: 0.32 s, tuned accent with a bounded streak pitch increase
- miss: 0.25 s, quiet descending accent
- peak: 0.56 s, four-note ascent
- collapse: 0.65 s, low rustling fall

Playback stays local, respects the phone's silent mode, mixes with other
media, and stops on pause/background/exit. Sound and haptics have independent
local switches. No microphone or background playback permission is added.
